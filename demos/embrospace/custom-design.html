Custom Embroidery Digitizing Service

We offer a custom digitizing service to help turn your personal image into a machine embroidery file. If you have your own design that you'd like to transform into embroidery - we're here to help!

You can submit your image or artwork by filling out our Custom Digitizing Form. We work with various formats, fabrics, and embroidery types - from simple logos to complex artistic illustrations.

How it works:

  • Step 1: Provide all necessary requirements and upload your image for digitizing;
  • Step 2: We'll analyze your artwork and select the optimal stitch structure, then begin the digitizing process;
  • Step 3: Once completed, you'll receive a PNG preview for approval. If needed, we'll promptly make any adjustments;
  • Step 4: After confirmation, we'll send the final embroidery file in your requested size and format, along with a PDF guide showing thread colors and step-by-step instructions.

We approach every order individually and guarantee high-quality digitizing.

You'll receive a quote within 24-48 hours after submitting your request.
